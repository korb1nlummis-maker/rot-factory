Extra self-test modules. Each file exports `default async (ctx) => { await ctx.T('area.name', async () => true | 'reason'); }`.

Satisfactory waves: name files `<area>_*.js` with test prefix `<area>.` (belts, split, power, build, furnish, transit, haul, arch, detector) and one `mp.<area>.` test per wave. `wave0_*.js` (prefixes `catalog.`, `wave0.`, `mp.catalog.`) covers the shared plumbing in `src/catalog.js` and `src/ext.js`: every catalog test should register its handlers by pushing into `TYPES` of `src/catalog.js` and removing them in a `finally`, never by editing a `catalog_*.js` file. See the Wave 0 section at the end of `DESIGN_SATISFACTORY.md`.

Build shell (wave 3): `build_*.js` (prefix `build.`) and `build_mp.js` (prefix `mp.build.`). `build_lib.js` has no default export; its `makeShell(ctx)` clears a test box in the open bay, aims, puts pieces down and removes every pad, wall and ramp afterwards (pads and walls are world cells, so a test that forgets would change what the next area sees).

Power parts (wave 2): `power_core.js` (solver: switches, breakers, priority shedding, storage, meter), `power_parts.js` (bench, placing, ports, settings, E, readouts, saves, M key) and `power_mp.js` (prefix `mp.power.`). `power_lib.js` has no default export; `makeKit(ctx)` shrinks the pole link to 4 m and reach to 3 m so several grids fit in the open bay, and builds grids of a pole, burning generators and 2 kW fans.

Belts (wave 1A): `belts_tiers.js` (marks, speed and power per tile, upgrade in place, corner arcs, bench rows, unlock upgrades, readouts), `belts_lifts.js`, `belts_ug.js`, `belts_plan.js` (planner), `belts_persist.js` (prefix `belts.`) and `belts_mp.js` (prefix `mp.belts.`; the older `belts_bin.js` and `mp_belts.js` cover the bend and the bin). `belts_lib.js` has no default export; `makeBeltKit(ctx)` lays belts, lifts and Lift Frames in the cleared bay, packs plush at the 0.34 spacing along a line and measures the rate into a vault, and its `T` wrapper puts back what a test leaves behind (planner, lift settings, a fake network role, floors).

Generator ladder and Hanging Lanterns: `gen_ladder.js` (prefix `gen.`: numbers, unlock chain, burn rule per rung, hoppers, readout, placing, ports, saves), `gen_mp.js` (`mp.gen.`), `hlamp_core.js` (`hlamp.`: aim and slots, one generator runs five, brownout dims the whole line, glow, readout, saves) and `hlamp_mp.js` (`mp.hlamp.`). They use `power_lib.js`. The audit of that wave is `audit_gens_burn.js` (energy conservation of fast plants at 60 and 30 fps and across lag spikes, readouts of very fast plants), `audit_gens_mp.js` (a guest cannot turn a cheap item into a generator, forged lantern places and cfg, hammer round trip) and `audit_gens_soak.js` (seeded random edits with invariants and a mid-run save and reload), all under `gens.audit.`.

Mergers and ruled splitters (wave 2B): `split_rules.js` (prefix `split.`: the pure rule resolution, rarity ranges, lanes, clean rules, no world), `split_belts.js` (fair merging, priority lanes, Smart and Programmable Splitters on real belts, overflow, order, default output, unpowered behavior, geometry of the cones and lanes, a random network that must conserve every plush), `split_parts.js` (unlocks and prices, placing and converting a belt, the hammer, cfg, copy and paste, the panel, power, readouts, saves, the look) and `split_mp.js` (`mp.split.`: sync, guest edits and placements through the host, forged commands, late joiner). `split_lib.js` has no default export; `makeSplitKit(ctx)` builds on the belt kit with `rig3` (three lines into a merger and a vault), `rigS` (one line into a splitter with a vault on each output), `part` (a part at a cell), `run` (the world for n seconds with a per step hook) and `pump` (a queue of plush into a tile).

Vehicle Scanner: `vscan_core.js` (`vscan.`: the unlock, the bench and its growing price, setting it down, forged requests, the sunk footings, power, readouts, settings, saves, text), `vscan_haul.js` (`vscan.`: trucks route through the nearest powered scanner, the clean scan, The One dumping the load with the alarm, the lane and the line, a truck that refuses to leave, diggers scooping The One and keeping it aboard, the borer, the sale guard, old routes and rows) and `vscan_mp.js` (`mp.vscan.`: ent+, late joiner, guest place and cfg, scan events and the 0.5 s row, the guest's E, truck states on the guest, the hammer). The haul tests build their own site with `site()` (a yard, a digger with a hopper, optionally holding The One) and drive the machines with a stepper that keeps every machine powered except the ones named in `off`.

Transit (wave 5): `transit_doors.js`, `transit_jump.js` (prefix `transit.`) and `transit_mp.js` (prefix `mp.transit.`). `transit_lib.js` has no default export; its `kit(ctx)` wraps the build shell kit (`makeShell`) and the power kit (`makeKit`), makes doors, lifts, call buttons, jump pads and cushion pads straight into the world (`door`, `lift`, `button`, `jump`, `cushion`, `platform`, `powerAt`) and its `clean()` takes every one of them down again with the cells they set and the cells they reserve. The real Player flies every jump (`stepPlayer`, `fly` in `transit_jump.js`), so "lands where previewed" and "no fall damage" are measured against `player.js`, not assumed. The 40 s limit per console call matters: run `__selftest('transit.')` and `__selftest('mp.transit.')` as two calls on a busy machine.

The audit of that wave: `audit_transit_cells.js` (`transit.audit.`: door panels cannot be taken by F or a guest `bulk` command, belts and machines keep out of doorways, shafts and pads, E in the car leaves a belt alone, chunk seams), `audit_transit_lift.js` (`transit.audit.lift-`: hand crank with no power, a car never runs through a cut shaft, crank and cut reach the guest), `audit_transit_soak.js` (`transit.audit.soak.`: four seeded random edit runs with the reserved-cell, door-row and item-conservation invariants and a real save and load, and a wandering real player in a town of one lift, a door and a pad) and `audit_transit_mp.js` (`mp.transit.audit.`: forged `place`, `cfg` and `decon` commands).
