CREW

What the crew do. Scrapper Bots dig tunnels into the pile, haul plush back to the bin and sell it. They grow as they work: more plush per trip, wider tunnels, faster hands.

Batteries. Every bot runs on a battery. Below 25% it walks to the nearest Charging Station that has charge. Feed a station Common to Epic plush. With no station the bot waits at the bin and trickles up very slowly, so build one.

Keys. T send the whole crew digging the way you face. Y call the whole crew home. V this panel. Or in the world: aim at a bot and press E to pick it, then aim at a target and press E (generator, charger, sorter, vault, belt, floor, bin, your feet to be followed, or your cart). Esc lets it go.

What the status words mean
  • Digging: at the face, taking plush. Heading out: walking to the face.
  • Hauling plush back: full, walking to the bin (or its drop-off). Unloading: selling at the bin.
  • Walking to / Recharging at a Charging Station: low battery, topping up from the station.
  • Waiting for a Charging Station: out of power with no station that has charge. It trickles up at the bin.
  • Following you, Waiting at the bin, Taking plush to its drop-off, Blocked: nothing more to dig that way.

HOW TO PLAY

The goal

The warehouse is full of millions of plush, and exactly one of them matters: Il Rotto Supremo. The Dossier computer shows what it looks like. Find it to win. Or give up and dig out of the exit, 4.9 km east.

Your first minutes

  1. Walk to the pile and look at a plush. Left click grabs it, instantly. Hold to keep grabbing until your hands are full.
  2. Walk near the SORT bin. It eats what you carry and pays you. Click again while holding plush to throw one in (the bin sucks thrown plush in).
  3. E at the desk (or Tab) buys upgrades: a bigger bag, faster hands, tools, crew. E at the bench crafts things.

Tools and the hotbar

Open the inventory with I. Put tools and building items on the 9-slot hotbar and use 1-9 to take one out; Q puts it away. An empty slot is your bare hands. With a tool out, click (or B) uses it. The hammer in slot 1 knocks down anything you built and gives it back.

Digging and staying alive

  • Dig tunnels by grabbing plush straight ahead of you. A tunnel stands for about 7 m on its own; the deeper and heavier the pile above, the shorter. Past that the roof creaks and falls.
  • Frames are 4x4 squares that carve their own section and hold the roof around them. Aim next to a frame and the next one snaps on any side. Struts and jacks are quick props.
  • Do not climb the piles. The higher you go, the likelier the slope slides under you.
  • Buried? You have an air countdown. R or right click punches plush out of the way (hold Space to punch up). Run out of air or health and you die, then wake on the bay floor with your hands empty. K uses a medkit.
  • Dust builds in tunnels. A respirator, vent fans and clean air help.

Automate it

Digging by hand is just the start. Hire Scrapper Bots (V) and send them digging (T). Build belts, sorters, vaults, mechs and borers on a generator and poles. Put Detector Gates on your lines: they beep green for ordinary plush and sound a red alarm, and stop the belt, if The One rolls through. Anything that reaches the bin without being scanned is sold, and The One cannot be bought back. Then spend your own time building tunnels and conveyors.
